Explicit Explanation
Session 7 · 05

No gaps. No overlaps. No exceptions.

A tessellation is a repeating pattern of shapes that covers a surface with no gaps and no overlaps.

To make the shapes fit, we use transformations: translation slides them, reflection flips them, rotation turns them.

Careful — not every repeated pattern is a tessellation. Gaps or overlaps? Then it's just a pattern.

Hexagons tiling with no gaps and no overlaps.
