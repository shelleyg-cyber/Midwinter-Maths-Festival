Explicit Explanation
Session 1 · 05

Three moves, three maths names

A slide is a…
Translation

The shape moves to a new position — no turning, no flipping.

A flip is a…
Reflection

The shape flips over a line — like a mirror image.

A turn is a…
Rotation

The shape turns around a point.

Every time, the shape stays the same size and same shape. Only where it is or which way it faces changes. When we moved shapes on the Cartesian plane — that was already a translation!
