Explicit Explanation
Session 2 · 05

Every point moves together

A translation means a slide. Every point on the shape moves the same distance in the same direction — no turning, no flipping, no stretching.

If a triangle moves 4 spaces right, every vertex moves 4 spaces right. The x-coordinates change; the y-coordinates stay the same.

Remember our Cartesian plane lessons? Moving shapes left, right, up and down — we were translating all along.

The dotted shape shows where it started.

Eyes up — one big idea

One vertex slid perfectly… but the rest of the shape went wobbly. What went wrong?

In a translation, every point moves the same distance in the same direction. The shape slides as one whole shape — check every vertex, not just one.
