Explicit Explanation
Session 6 · 05

The tool moves it — you explain it

In GeoGebra you will create a polygon, then use the tools to translate it (slide), reflect it (flip across a line) and rotate it (turn around a point).

The important mathematics is not clicking the tool — it's recording and describing what happened: "The shape moved 4 units right." "The shape flipped across the y-axis." "The shape turned a quarter turn around a point."

Sentence starters: I translated… I reflected… I rotated…

The dotted shape shows where it started.
