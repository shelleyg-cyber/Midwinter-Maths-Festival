Explicit Explanation
Session 4 · 05

Three things describe every turn

A rotation means a turn around a fixed point called the centre of rotation. The shape stays the same size and shape — its orientation changes.

Every rotation is described by three things: the turning point, the direction (clockwise or anticlockwise), and the size of the turn — quarter, half or full.

A quarter turn clockwise means every vertex turns the same amount around the centre point — watch the demo's marked dot.

The dotted shape shows where it started.

Spotlight
Session 4 · 09

Eyes up — one big idea

It definitely moved… but did it actually turn?

A rotation must turn around a point. The shape does not slide across or flip over. It stays the same size and shape, but faces a different direction.
