Explicit Explanation
Session 3 · 05

The mirror line never lies

A reflection means a flip. The shape flips across a line of reflection — same size, same shape, facing the opposite way.

The line works like a mirror: every point on the reflected shape is the same distance from the line as the original point, just on the other side.

On the Cartesian plane, reflecting across an axis changes the coordinates in a predictable way. Today the focus is the flip — and keeping the shape accurate.

The dotted shape shows where it started.

Flip it together

2Mark each matching point the same distance from the line — on the other side.
3Reconnect the points to draw the reflected shape.

Eyes up — one big idea

Did that shape really flip… or did it just scoot across the line?

A reflection is not just moving to the other side. It must flip like a mirror image — and each matching point must be the same distance from the line of reflection.
