Explicit Explanation
Session 8 · 05

A pattern is only half the job

To create a tessellation, repeat your shape carefully so the copies fit together: translation slides it into place, reflection flips it, rotation turns it.

The finished pattern matters — but the explanation matters just as much. Describe how the shapes were moved, and why the pattern tessellates.

Think aloud like a designer: "I translated this shape across the row. Then I reflected the next row so the shapes fitted together."

Triangles tiling with no gaps and no overlaps.
